# Search terms and target employers, by job family

Use every title in English AND Spanish (and French for Paris) where sensible.
Companies are starting points, not limits. Always look for more like them.

## 1. Consulting / strategy (Simon-Kucher-style)
Titles: Associate Consultant, Junior Consultant, Analyst, Pricing Analyst, Strategy Consultant,
Business Analyst (consulting), Graduate Consultant, Consultor Junior, Analista de Estrategia
Employers: Simon-Kucher, Roland Berger, Oliver Wyman, Bain, BCG, McKinsey, LEK, Kearney, Arthur D. Little,
Strategy&, Deloitte, PwC, EY, KPMG, Accenture Strategy, Capgemini Invent, Analysys Mason, Oxera,
Ayming, Everis/NTT DATA, Alten, Esade/IESE-linked consultancies, boutique strategy firms in Barcelona

## 2. Economic research / market intelligence
Titles: Research Analyst, Market Intelligence Analyst, BI Analyst, Economic Analyst, Competitive Intelligence Analyst,
Market Research Analyst, Consultant Research & Insights, Strategy & Insights Analyst, Industry Analyst
Employers: Kantar, Ipsos, NielsenIQ/GfK, IQVIA, Euromonitor, S&P Global, Moody's, Oxford Economics, Statista,
Frost & Sullivan, NERA, Compass Lexecon, Control Risks, Eurasia Group, Teneo, Oxford Analytica

International Relations Officer, Researcher, Project Officer, Partnerships Officer, External Relations Officer,

IBEI, Barcelona City Council (international affairs), Area Metropolitana de Barcelona (international relations

UNDP/UNEP/FAO/IOM/UNHCR (roster and consultancy postings), ReliefWeb, Devex, UN Careers, EU CV Online

"programme officer Barcelona", "EU affairs Barcelona"

## 10. Tech-adjacent
Titles: Customer Success Manager (junior), Solutions Consultant, Sales Engineer (junior), Product Operations,
Business Analyst, BI Analyst, Data Analyst (business side), Implementation Specialist, Technical Account Manager (junior),
Community/Programme Manager at tech companies
Employers: any Barcelona tech scale-up (see Family 5), plus Zendesk, HubSpot, Salesforce, ServiceNow, Workday, Personio, Miro, Notion

## 11. Sales / BD (B2B)
Titles: SDR, BDR, Account Executive (junior/mid), Business Development Associate, Account Manager (B2B tech/services),
Inside Sales, Sales Development, Commercial Analyst
Rule: prefer tech, SaaS, consulting, energy, logistics, MENA-facing. Skip FMCG/on-trade/retail.
Arabic- and French-speaking sales roles for EMEA/MENA territories are especially good.

## Job sources to sweep every run (WebSearch + WebFetch)
Company career pages (first choice, freshest and least crowded), LinkedIn Jobs public listings, Glassdoor, Indeed,
InfoJobs, Infoempleo, JobLeads, jobsinbarcelona.es, Barcelona Activa, Tecnoempleo, Welcome to the Jungle,
Otta/Welcome, Wellfound, Devex, ReliefWeb, UN Careers, EU CV Online / EPSO, Euro-jobs (EuroBrussels, EurActiv Jobs),
Idealist, Work in Startups, Talent.com, Adzuna, Jooble, Jobtoday, Glassdoor Barcelona, Ecrjobs, Barcelona Tech City,
Job boards for consultancies (Consulting Quest, Management Consulted), and recruiter agency pages (Michael Page,
Hays, Robert Half, Randstad, Adecco, Page Personnel, Kelly, Talent Brand, Synergie).
Also search Google-style: `"associate" "Barcelona" site:greenhouse.io`, `site:lever.co`, `site:ashbyhq.com`,
`site:workable.com`, `site:smartrecruiters.com`, `site:personio.de` (these are the applicant-tracking systems many
Barcelona companies use, and their postings are often not on the big boards).
