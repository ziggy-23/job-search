# Veeva Associate Consultant: decode, gaps, extras

Source: my first fetch of the posting (summary only). I did not re-read the full text, so check the live page for exact wording before you submit.

## What they want
Must-haves: recent graduate (max 2 yrs post-grad), curiosity for technology and problem-solving, strong analytical and critical thinking, excellent written and verbal communication, English fluency, EU work authorisation, live near Barcelona (hybrid 2-3 days), 20-30% travel.
Not required: consulting or life sciences experience.
Start: April or September 2027.

## Gaps and how to bridge
- **Life sciences / pharma knowledge:** none. Not required. Read Veeva's product pages (CRM, Vault) and one pharma commercial-operations overview in the next two weeks. Interview line: "I haven't worked in life sciences, but I learn a new sector fast: I did it for climate risk and Yemen's WASH and health sectors at GlobalCAD."
- **Technology consulting / software implementation:** no direct experience. The nearest truthful evidence is the Excel scoring models, the AI tender-screening automation and the Interreg monitoring tool. Do not call these "implementations".
- **Python and SQL:** the bank says confirm real tasks before claiming. Left off the CV. Add them only if you can talk through a real query or script.
- **Power BI level:** unconfirmed, so it is listed but not detailed. Be ready to describe what you built.
- **Post-grad window:** BA finished 2024, MA courses ended June 2026. Check how Veeva counts "max 2 years" (it may count from the BA). Ask or note the MA on the form.
- **Start date:** the programme starts April or September 2027. Your GlobalCAD contract ends Nov 2026, so there is a gap. Decide what to do in between (a bridge job or the C-tier roles in the tracker).
